Why Join Us 活動亮點
Discover New Taipei Like a Local
為什麼要來？

Each route is crafted by young Cultural Ambassadors — locals who've trained all year to share New Taipei's living culture with the world, in both English and Chinese.

🗣️
Bilingual Guided Tour
雙語深度導覽
Full commentary in both English and Chinese — every story, every detail, accessible to all.
中英文同步解說，讓每位參與者都能深入了解在地故事。
🎨
Hands-On Experience
親手文化體驗
Roll up your sleeves and create something with local artisans. Crafts that carry real stories.
親自動手製作在地特色工藝，感受職人的技藝與溫度。
🎁
Take a Souvenir Home
專屬紀念品帶回家
Every route includes a craft you'll make yourself — a memory you can hold in your hands.
每條路線都有專屬手作體驗，帶走獨一無二的新北記憶。
